About JCDC

Jeddah Central Development Company (JCDC) is a Public Investment Fund (PIF) company dedicated to developing world-class destinations in Jeddah. We blend the city's cultural heritage with forward-looking urban development to enhance quality of life and create new opportunities for communities.
