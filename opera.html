Opera House

The Opera House features three performance venues designed for opera, orchestral concerts, and theatrical productions, serving artists and audiences throughout Saudi Arabia.

Location

Beach, Leisure & Lifestyle District

Capacity

2,463 seats

Total GFA

21,151 m²

Performance Venue and Cultural Programming

Designed by Henning Larsen Architects with Buro Happold engineering, the Opera House provides three specialized performance spaces for opera, orchestral music, theater, and community programming. The waterfront landmark incorporates elements from Jeddah's architectural heritage within a contemporary design.

The facility supports full-scale opera productions, concerts, and theatrical performances while offering flexible programming for local artists and international touring productions.

Key Highlights of the Museum

Lyrical Theater 1,573 seats

Main performance hall designed for opera, orchestral concerts, and large-scale productions.

Mid-Scale Theater 707 seats

Flexible venue for chamber music, plays, and community performances.

Rehearsal Theater 183 seats

Intimate space for rehearsals, workshops, and experimental programming.
