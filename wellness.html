Wellness District

A pedestrian-friendly district focused on healthcare and wellness.

Healthcare and Wellness Hub

The Wellness District is designed to create a supportive healthcare environment with complementary wellness services and facilities. The district connects to Ribbon Park and Central Park, providing healthcare workers and visitors with access to recreational spaces and outdoor wellness areas.

The district features wellness services, fitness facilities, and support amenities that serve the hospital community and surrounding neighborhoods. Al-Balad architectural elements are incorporated throughout the district with therapeutic landscaping designed to support healing and recovery.
