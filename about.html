About Us

We develop world-class destinations where heritage meets contemporary living, creating vibrant communities that celebrate culture.

Our Story

Jeddah Central Development Company (JCDC), a Public Investment Fund (PIF) company was established in 2019 and is dedicated to developing world-class destinations that shape Jeddah's urban future.

As the master developer of the 5.7-million-square-meter Jeddah Central waterfront destination, JCDC leads every phase - from planning and execution to governance and operations - ensuring excellence in design, innovation, and sustainability.

Guided by a strong governance framework and supported by 10+ ISO certifications across quality, environment, health and safety, business continuity, and information security, JCDC is redefining waterfront living and celebrating Jeddah's cultural identity through landmark developments that create lasting value for the city and its people.

Vision

To open the world’s eyes and hearts to Saudi through celebrating its heritage and contemporary culture.

Mission

To provide a gift to the Jeddah Community by creating an unparalleled waterfront destination of real estate, leisure and entertainment accessible to all residents and visitors.
