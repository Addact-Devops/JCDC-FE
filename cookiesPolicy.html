Cookies Policy

We Use Cookies

JCDC values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in compliance with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and other relevant laws. This privacy notice informs you about how we collect, use, protect, retain, and disclose your personal data.

What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to store data that can be recalled by a web server in the domain that placed the cookie. We use cookies to remember your preferences and settings, provide a personalized experience, and analyze how our website is used.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies: Necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off.

Performance Cookies: Allow us to count visits and traffic sources, helping us measure and improve the performance of our site.

Functional Cookies: Enable the website to provide enhanced functionality and personalization.

Advertising Cookies: Used to make advertising messages more relevant to you.

Your Choices

You have the right to accept or decline cookies. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can usually modify your browser setting to decline cookies if you prefer. However, if you choose to decline cookies, certain features of our website may not function as intended. You can also manage or withdraw your cookie consent at any time by adjusting your preferences through the cookie settings on our website or in your browser.

Consent

By continuing to browse JCDC website, you consent to the use of cookies on your device for enhancing your site experience and for analytical purposes.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about our use of cookies, please contact us at privacy@jcd.com.sa
