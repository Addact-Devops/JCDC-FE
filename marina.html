Marina District

A waterfront district featuring a 473-berth marina, luxury cruise terminal, and mixed-use residential and hospitality developments.

Marina and Waterfront Living

Marina District houses a 473-berth marina and luxury cruise terminal serving regional and international vessels. The district combines waterfront residential buildings with hospitality facilities, retail spaces, and marine services, all connected by pedestrian pathways along the Red Sea coastline.

Architecture incorporates Jeddah's maritime heritage through contemporary coastal design, creating integrated mixed-use developments that serve both residents and visitors.

Invest in Jeddah Central

Discover investment opportunities in residential, hospitality, retail, wellness, and commercial within a world-class waterfront destination. Anchored by iconic landmarks, Jeddah Central delivers sustainable growth and long-term value.
