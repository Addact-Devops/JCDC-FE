Stadium

The landmark features a retractable roof, climate-controlled seating, and is integrated with four surrounding villages offering activities beyond match days.

FIFA-Compliant Sports and Entertainment Venue

Designed by Gerkan, Marg & Partners Architects (GMP), the stadium hosts international matches, league events, and entertainment programs. The climate-responsive venue features advanced infrastructure to support year-round operations in Jeddah's climate.

Four integrated villages surrounding the stadium create a comprehensive destination with dining, retail, wellness, and community facilities. This design ensures the venue serves the community beyond match days, supporting daily activities and special events.
