Privacy Policy

Clause 1: Introduction

JCDC values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in compliance with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and other relevant laws. This privacy notice informs you about how we collect, use, protect, retain, and disclose your personal data.

Clause 2: The data that will be collected.

Personal data that will be collected include but is not limited to:

Personal details: Name

Contact Details: Phone number and email address

Technical Information: IP address, cookies, browser type, and OS version. (Refer to our Cookies Notice for more details)

Clause 3: Data Collection Methods:

JCDC may collect personal data through various means, including but not limited to online platforms, forms, subscriptions, in-person interactions, cookies and tracking technologies, web analytics service tools, and other direct or indirect communication channels.

Clause 4: Purpose of collection and usage of Personal Data

JCDC collects and processes personal data for the purpose of maintaining communication with individuals, which may include, but is not limited to, providing and enhancing our services, sending updates, marketing materials, invitations, seasonal greetings, gifts newsletters, investment opportunities, promotional content, and other relevant information that aligns with JCDC's objectives.

Personal data will be used solely for the purposes outlined, in accordance with PDPL requirements.

Clause 5: Storage and Destruction of Personal Data

Personal data is securely hosted and processed within JCDC systems. JCDC employs security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction and ensure proper communication and action is carried out to the best of its ability under normal circumstances. Your data will be retained as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this notice and in compliance with PDPL requirements.

Clause 6: Legal Basis for Collecting and Processing Personal Data.

The collection and processing of personal data at JCDC is based on the explicit consent of the individual concerned. At any time, you have the right to withdraw this consent, unless there is an alternate legal justification for the data processing. To withdraw consent or for any related queries, please contact the Cyber and Information Security Department at JCDC. Email: privacy@jcd.com.sa.

Clause 7: Rights of the Personal Data Subject

Under the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), you are entitled to the following rights regarding the management of your personal data:

  • - Right to be informed: you have the right to be informed about the collection and use of your personal data, including the purposes, legal basis, data retention periods, and your rights regarding your data.
  • - Right of access to Personal Data: You have the right to access your Personal Data.
  • - Right to Request Access to Personal Data: You may request a copy of your personal data by contacting us through the email specified in Clause 9.
  • - Right to Request Correction of Personal Data: If you find any of your personal data to be
inaccurate, incorrect, or incomplete, you have the right to request its correction. - Right to Request Destruction of Personal Data: You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data under certain conditions, except where legal provisions dictate a specific retention period or if contractual obligations require otherwise. - Right to Withdraw Consent for Data Processing:

You may withdraw your consent for the processing of your personal data or object to its processing at any time, provided there are no legitimate reasons requiring its continued processing. For detailed instructions on how to exercise these rights, please refer to Clause 9.

Clause 8: Sharing/Exchanging Personal Data

We will not share or exchange your personal data with other parties, except when necessary to send seasonal greetings or gifts, or as required by law.

Clause 9: Exercising the Rights of the Personal Data Subject

The personal data subject has the right to request access/ correction/destruction of his/her data by communicating via the following.

Mail to: Cyber and Information Security Department.

Email: privacy@jcd.com.sa

Clause 11: Complaints and Inquiries

If there are any complaints or inquiries related to the Privacy Notice or dealing with personal data, please contact the Cyber and Information Security Department at JCDC. Email: privacy@jcd.com.sa.
