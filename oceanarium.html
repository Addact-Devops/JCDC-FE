Marine Education and Conservation Center

Designed by Skidmore, Owings & Merrill (SOM), the Oceanarium provides marine education through interactive exhibits focused on Red Sea ecosystems and conservation. The facility features seven themed pavilions offering hands-on learning about coastal biodiversity, coral reef systems, and marine research.

The center supports environmental education programs, coral restoration research, and public engagement with marine conservation efforts.
