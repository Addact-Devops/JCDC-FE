Welcome to Jeddah Central

Jeddah Central spans 5.7 million square meters of Red Sea waterfront, anchored by four architectural landmarks. The destination will feature ten attractions across six districts that combine culture, hospitality, retail, and waterfront recreation for residents and visitors.

In the heart of Jeddah, Jeddah Central offers easy access to the city’s major points of interest

Six Districts. One Destination.

Each district in Jeddah Central serves a distinct purpose; from cultural expression and wellness to entertainment, retail, and everyday living. Together, they form a unified destination built on walkability, sustainability, and human-centered design.
